Breathe With Eli

My own breathwork practice
Design, copy, build, and the business underneath

Breathe With Eli is my own practice. I facilitate breathwork sessions and run a men’s group in Rochester, and this site is how people find the work, learn what it is, and book a seat. It is also where every idea gets tested first, because when something breaks here it breaks in my own calendar.

The site opens on a single line with a slow ripple spreading around it, and nothing else competing for attention. The palette is bone and deep green, and the type is small.

The opening is not fixed. Arrive again and the line has changed, along with the ring of marks around it.

I built it by hand rather than on a website builder: plain files, no subscription platform underneath. The booking runs against my real calendar, payments are built in, and the mailing list is mine. It is the same foundation I build for clients, tested here first.
